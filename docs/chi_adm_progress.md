# China Branch progress

## M7 presentation and discovery

Evidence was independent of Imperial Security before the rework and remains independent. Qing owns the single active case. The founder sets evidence to 10 through `chi_adm_qing_security_found`. The country pulse calls `chi_adm_qing_security_court_review`, which adds `chi_adm_qing_security_monthly_evidence`: 1 base, 2 per eligible armed office (including the claimant), 1 per eligible covert office, and 1 extra per armed office at 70 or more Corruption. Pledges change subsequent monthly evidence. Promotion to phase 3 at month six or later with two valid armed governors sets a minimum of 50. Betrayal adds 30, an inquiry adds 25 to an active prewar case, and pressure adds 15 after discovery. Evidence is capped at 100 and cleared on closure. No effect subtracts from the running total.

One reachable route is a claimant who remains eligible and a second eligible governor who joins in phase 2 after month four. At the month-six review, two valid armed governors advance the case to phase 3 and raise evidence to at least 50. A single eligible claimant also produces 3 per month and can reach 52 at month 14, before the 30-month expiry. A recruited governor can expose a case by reporting once at least 20 evidence has accumulated.

`chi_adm_qing_security_brief` dispatches the discovery notification once at 50. Founding does not dispatch it. Imperial Security activates for a valid Qing court when `chi_adm_qing_security_court_threat` is true: a prewar case at 50 or more, an open phase-five contest at 50 or more, or a pending restoration settlement. Loss of eligibility deactivates the entry. Closure clears evidence, case state and the briefing marker; pending restoration keeps the court actions available until resolved or expired.

The inquiry lives in Governance of the Empire. Public warnings can display the option. The player and AI share the same authorization check: an active prewar case, evidence below 100, spare Bureaucracy and no current inquiry. The effect rechecks these conditions before committing Bureaucracy and adding up to 25 evidence. The AI still requires public warnings and uses the existing Qing monthly pulse.

The provincial journal presents Provincial Political Affairs until the governor is the claimant, has access to recruitment, sees an exposed case, or has a wartime pact. The existing dossier and roster permissions still control its detailed information.

### Validation

Focused static source and rule checks cover startup, founding, hidden monthly accumulation, discovery, inquiry with and without a case, betrayal, AI routing, and closure. Save/load and two-human secrecy still require runtime checks. No M1–M8 validator suite was present in this checkout.

## Chinese Missions and the Heavenly Kingdom expansion

### Implemented

- The Chinese Missions JE retains its access and missionary counter. Its opening mission and five monthly draws now dispatch to the selected state's current owner with the existing choices. The central draw advances the counter once. Receipt checks ownership and cancels stale events. Provincial access restrictions and missionary fear block re-opening on the existing monthly pulse. Provincial permission broadens access while an imperial mission JE is active and within imperial prohibitions. Regulation preserves limited access and rules out the unrestricted event choices. Imperial expulsion still closes the central system and its marked access.
- Provincial Religious Affairs appears under the Governor's Accounts. It displays current mission rules, local state counts, Protestant and turmoil thresholds, and relations with Qing. Governors can independently choose mission policy and handle the Society. Investigation risks failure and commits Authority; supervised missions cost Bureaucracy. Tolerance, paid covert aid, formal defection, temporary neutrality, and withdrawing a commitment are separate choices. Relief uses the existing Imperial Memorials approval, refusal and compromise. AI reviews weigh religious demographics, Loyalty, Autonomy, turmoil, Devout participation, available resources, and whether a general is available.
- The uprising still needs eight eligible footholds. Spread uses one realm-wide adjacency predicate for eligibility and selection. It works between Qing and its administrations and across administrative borders. The founding JE prefers a separate eligible state outside Nanjing where one exists. The event grants Nanjing its own foothold under Qing or an administration and performs its Protestant conversion once. Capital protections are unchanged.
- Uncommitted and Qing-loyal governors retain the usual subject war obligation. Paid covert aid refreshes one local foothold and can fund the Heavenly Kingdom without switching government. Consenting defectors and neutral governors must be at peace and outside existing committed diplomatic plays. Before war, the script snapshots and removes their original Qing pacts. It removes defectors from the Qing backer side before adding them as Taiping backers; detached neutrals receive no backer assignment. A failed play creation attempts to restore detached pacts. At settlement, eligible neutrals restore their original pact if Qing survives, while defectors are left independent. A governor retains their country unless they voluntarily take command of Taiping.
- Qing receives the first takeover choice. On decline, one eligible human governor is invited at a time in this fixed order: ZHI, AHU, HNA, YUE, FRS, XIB, YUN, SIH, SHN, SHA, MGL, MCH, XIN. Eligibility comes from a local foothold, lost land, covert support, or a pledge. Taiping stores the pending recipient; the province stores that it was offered the choice. An invitation advances on decline/default; the Qing war JE can resume the queue if an invitee disappears or leaves player control. Acceptance rechecks recipient, live war, AI control and the claim flag. The sole switching effect reserves Taiping before running native `play_as`. Switching does not alter the former province's existing alignment or ownership.
- The Heavenly Court adds ten conditional events covering the King's authority, the commanders' council and a contingent Tianjing command crisis, land-system proposals, moving the capital to Nanjing, expedition logistics, parish schooling and religious conformity, relations with foreign envoys, and the staffing and repair of postwar government. A postwar decision can reopen prefectural offices in an incorporated state with turmoil. Existing Taiping laws, characters, ideologies, military setup and A Kingdom of Believers remain in use.
- Victory requires enforced annexation between the two principals in the Heavenly Civil War. Qing restores recorded ownership where the original administration remains eligible, and restores original incorporation for the states recovered. Taiping victory retains the existing postwar event and conversion JE. A truce or interruption grants neither victory, leaves negotiated ownership as settled, and keeps state records for an eventual valid final settlement. The delayed native war-end hook cleans up temporary pact state.

## Provincial Reform and Modernization

### Synchronization and ownership

The original game-start and monthly Qing coordination called `chi_qing_share_army_reforms`. It copied Qing's army-stage variable, armament penalty, four Mandate penalty bands, addiction and defeat to eligible subjects. The defeat copy used an untimed modifier while Qing's original penalty was active. Qing's early army JE also served as the gate for removing the subjects' military penalties when the court finished.

The court still shares technologies, national military-law unlocks, Consolidated Authority, and its existing economic, welfare and rebellion effects. The army count, armaments, Fragmented Rule, addiction and defeat now belong to the province. Low, mid, high and max Mandate centralization bands contain provincial-capacity penalties and are represented locally by one Fragmented Rule tier. The court continues to receive its own Mandate modifiers and bonuses. No provincial commission writes to the court's Mandate or finishes a Qing journal entry.

Each province keeps its milestone count, pending monthly reports, opium campaign choices and the latest defeat it received. A versioned first pass samples the old local modifiers before cleanup. It preserves existing stages and respects absent penalties on already-synchronized saves. Opening administrations fall back to the starting penalties if the court JEs have yet to initialize. A genuinely new administration uses Qing's current conditions unless an older record for its tag exists. The archive restores progress and paid work when an administration is re-created or released. Independence, secession or conversion pauses commissions and releases their Bureaucracy while keeping the penalties and progress on the country. Rejoining resumes the work. Integration or annexation does not transfer the province's reform to the annexing country. A winning revolution can inherit the country's variables through the normal engine path.

### Self-Strengthening, Qing support and AI

Military and Education support army work. Military and Economy support armaments. Administration and Education support the yamen. Administration and Economy support suppression and recovery. An owned tier I or II project saves two reports and an owned tier III project saves four. The relevant completed Fuzhou, Hanyang or Jiangnan regional works also qualify for the four-report reduction while their existing project modifier is active. The reduction is per commission and never removes an entire penalty. Several M6 pathways also satisfy a stage requirement.

The existing relief memorial transfers £20,000 from Qing on approval or £10,000 on an accepted compromise. Either outcome shortens the next commission begun within a year by two reports. The governor still pays the listed commission cost and can proceed independently. AI governors use the same affordability and prerequisite checks, fund at most one commission at a time, and typically keep a reserve buffer. War raises the priority of the army, high turmoil raises suppression, autonomy raises administrative reform, and recent aid raises recovery. M7 conspiracy eligibility and recruitment are unchanged.

### Imperial defeat clock

The existing defeat option still applies the national 7,300-day decaying modifier and runs its original effects. It now registers a defeat number and a single global expiry flag for the provincial consequences. Present provinces receive one decaying modifier. Late entrants receive only the estimated time left, with their initial strength scaled to that time. The weekly clock is rounded conservatively, so the estimate can be up to 12 days long. The global expiry flag ends the penalty at the next provincial weekly check even if the late entrant's native modifier still has days left. Monthly coordination never refreshes the modifier. Early recovery records the defeat number, so the province cannot receive it again for the same defeat. Defeat modifiers in older saves have no recorded start date. Those provinces use Qing's existing modifier as the expiry signal and do not get a new 20-year term. The legacy fallback does not reconstruct the old decay curve.
