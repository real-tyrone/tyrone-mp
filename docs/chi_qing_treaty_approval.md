# Russian treaty approval

All three eastern treaty buttons now submit requests to CHI, with one pending request at a time. Human ownership no longer excludes a donor. Approval consumes a Russian-held obligation on Qing; that obligation hides refusal. AI acceptance is guaranteed at Neutral relations or better (>= -19); at worse relations, acceptance is weighted by Russian army projection against Qing plus all subjects, existing war and particularly bad relations. No land is transferred by the initial Russian click.
